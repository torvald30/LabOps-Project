Phishing Awareness Analysis

The demonstration shows a simple login page that could be used to explain phishing risks.

Five warning signs:

1. Suspicious or incorrect website URL.
2. Missing HTTPS security.
3. No clear company or website identity.
4. Poor design or unusual language.
5. Unexpected requests for login information.

Protection methods:

1. Check the website address carefully.
2. Use trusted bookmarks.
3. Enable multi-factor authentication.
4. Never enter passwords on suspicious websites.
5. Report suspicious messages and pages.
